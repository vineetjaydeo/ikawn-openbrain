# Ruhi — User Guide

## What is OpenBrain?

OpenBrain is your direct line to Ruhi — iKawn's intelligent commerce copilot. Think of it as a dedicated conversation space where you can talk to Ruhi about anything related to your brand, business, or creative needs.

Everything you discuss here becomes part of Ruhi's knowledge. When Ruhi is fully connected to iKawn OS (os.ikawn.com), she'll carry everything she learned here into her autonomous work — campaign planning, content generation, brand voice, and more.

## Getting Started

1. **Start a conversation** — Click "New Chat" or just start typing
2. **Ask anything** — Brand strategy, creative direction, product questions, business analysis
3. **Attach files** — Paste images, upload PDFs, share documents for Ruhi to analyze
4. **Search the web** — Ruhi automatically searches when she needs current information
5. **Share links** — Paste a URL and Ruhi will read and analyze the content

## Tips for Better Conversations

- **Be specific about your brand**: Tell Ruhi about your brand voice, target audience, and visual style. She remembers everything.
- **Teach through correction**: If Ruhi's tone isn't right, tell her. She learns from corrections.
- **Use Pro mode for complex tasks**: Toggle to PRO for deeper analysis, strategy work, and creative briefs.
- **Build context over time**: The more you talk to Ruhi, the better she understands your business.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Enter** — Send message
- **Shift + Enter** — New line
- **Paste image** — Attach directly from clipboard

## Privacy & Security

- All conversations are private to your account
- Ruhi never shares information between users
- Files are stored securely in encrypted cloud storage
- Ruhi will never reveal technical details about her architecture
